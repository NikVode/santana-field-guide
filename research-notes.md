## Group tools worth knowing (added Oct 15)

- Ready Check from the team panel (leader).
- AFK detection and penalty on matchmade teams only, not premades.
- Quick Request / Adjust Tank Role: posts your request to team chat for the leader to confirm. Nameless Sword users aren't auto-assigned tank.
- Claim All Rewards in Hero's Realm (except appearance chests).
- Multiplayer Wooden Stake: leader pulls the team in, live team and individual damage panel. Good for checking rotations before a run.
- Boss Dummy in the Sword Trial training ground (Timed, Fixed-HP, Free).
- Dungeon Guide button on the Sword Trial and Hero's Realm screens opens the game's own written walkthrough.
- Shield % now shows on some bosses; VFX and umbrella transparency settings help with clutter.
- Fledgling Protection: in matched runs, new players get extra revives, and whoever is matched with them gets extra revives and Never-Delay Points.

### Compass & Chime (Lv 100): Lodestar

The arena has four chain pillars in the corners. Assign two chain players before the pull.

- Charge: yellow line shows his rush. Two players stretch a chain across the path. He hits it, gets stunned and takes an armor debuff.
- Flaming Flying Sword: the targeted player runs to the arena edge. When the countdown (Intercept) starts, connect two chains across the sword's flight path. One chain won't stop it.
- World Ablaze: he shields up and dashes around the edge, gaining stacking damage each lap. Block the dashes with chains, then burn the shield.
- Flaming Swordcut: golden circles under targeted players. Move them away from the pillars, or that pillar is on fire and unusable for 25 seconds.
- Charge Strike: he goes invincible and winds up a raid-wide hit. Two players attach the chains from the pillars in front of him directly to his body.
- Airborne phase: he covers a pillar in an iron shell. Everyone breaks the shell, then attach all four chains to pull him down.

### Compass & Chime: Bell Toller

Bronze bells can be rotated, knocked over or stood up.

- Call of the Bell: drops a bell on a random player. Everyone breaks it to free them. Then knock that bell over.
- Resonant Toll: he strikes every bell. Upright bells blast the whole arena; knocked-over bells fire a line where they face. Knock everything over first, and dodge the lines. Anyone still trapped in a bell dies, so free teammates first.
- Endless Echo: a bronze horse charges a random player. Turn a bell's opening toward the boss, then the target baits the horse into that bell. The soundwave interrupts the wipe.
- Terrestrial Drumbeat: big shield plus a wipe timer. Aim bell openings at him so their soundwaves break the shield. After it breaks he takes 70% less damage for a while, so hold your burst.
- Take My Hammer → No One Runs!: numbers over heads show the hammer throw order (top damage first). Stand bells up and stand next to them so each throw smashes a bell; higher numbers stand farther out. If he absorbs more than one bell in No One Runs!, it's a wipe.

Rewards: Tier 100 Path Chests, Tier 100 materials, Inner Way Note custom chests, Sword Trial appearance chests, chance at Melodic and Attunement Stones (Dustless). Compass and Chime Chests (Chivalry Voucher Exchange) can drop the Shattered Crest and Brainy Bell appearances.

### Segmented Puppets (Lv 96): Ironheart Centipede + Ci and Cuo

Neither boss dies to raw damage; both are pure mechanics checks. The trial Centipede shares a model with the world boss version but fights completely differently, so ignore world-boss habits. Claiming rewards costs 60 Energy.

**Assign before the pull**

| Who | Ironheart Centipede | Ci and Cuo |
|---|---|---|
| Players 1–2 | Grab an arm, kill Centipede Remains at the edges | Mask markers on the far side |
| Players 3–4 | Grab an arm, stand in the Repeated Crushing lane and interrupt | Mask markers near the center and the side stages |
| Player 5 | Deflect and damage; pick up a dead player's arm | Call colors, watch for same-color collisions |

**Ironheart Centipede**

- The bar to the right of its HP is a parry gauge. Deflecting its red attacks drains it. When it empties, the Centipede goes into Disarm Paralysis and drops its bladed arms. Start deflecting from the pull; don't wait on cooldowns.
- Crushing Roll (red line AoE): the best gauge drain. Deflect it.
- Hunting: marks one player and fires ten fast slashes at them. Everyone faces the boss and deflects through it.
- Arms drop: four players pick up one arm each. They're single-use tools for the next two mechanics. One per person, no hoarding.
- Venom Leak: arena-wide damage plus stacking poison, and Centipede Remains spawn at the room's edges and crawl toward the middle. Players 1–2 walk up and use the interact button with the arm to destroy them. Any Remain that reaches the boss gets absorbed.
- Repeated Crushing: gold shield, then it rolls down a rectangular lane. Players 3–4 stand in the lane before it starts and interact to stop it. Sprinting in late is how this fails.
- Golden poison pools: dodge only. Gold attacks can never be deflected.
- If the disarm is slow: it stacks Toxic Infusion, which buffs its Predation and Bite. At full stacks it pulls players in for lethal damage, and every extra Toxic cast fails a talent.

| Talent | Condition |
|---|---|
| 1-1 | Destroy a Centipede Remain with a dropped arm at least once |
| 1-2 | Interrupt Repeated Crushing at least once |
| 1-3 | Interrupt the first hit of every Repeated Crushing and let no Remains be absorbed |
| 2-1 | 15 deflects during the first armed phase |
| 2-2 | 10 deflects during Hunting without taking a hit |
| 2-3 | Disarm in under 1 minute, with fewer than 3 Toxic casts and fewer than 2 Venom Leaks |

**Ci and Cuo**

- At the start: six small dancer puppets and raised side stages around the edge. You can break up to two puppets before they go immune, which means fewer colors to juggle later.
- Dance, My Friend: targeted players get a gold circle and a red or blue mask. Run to a puppet of the opposite color (it starts chasing you), then lead it into its opposite-colored partner. They merge into a pair. Pairs keep the boss from powering up.
- No opposite puppet left? Drag the charging puppet onto an empty side stage to park it.
- Never let two same-color puppets touch. Watch your running line; a charging puppet hits whatever's in front of it.
- Get every puppet where it's going within 10 seconds.
- Hand in Hand: a pair aims a charge lane at a player. Stand so the lane points at another pair and the charge knocks both down. Do this early: you need four pairs alive when the boss dies.
- Thousandfold Swords: wide rotating gold lines. Dodge only.
- Dance between Flowers: players inside the red circle deflect to interrupt it. If it goes off, the outer ring of the room is safe, and survivors get a chain-blade debuff.

| Talent | Condition |
|---|---|
| 1-1 | Knock down a pair with another pair's charge |
| 1-2 | No damage from Thousandfold Swords |
| 1-3 | Kill the boss with at least four pairs on the field |
| 2-1 | Park a puppet on a side stage at least once |
| 2-2 | Never let two same-color puppets connect |
| 2-3 | Every puppet guided within 10 seconds; no player hit more than 5 times |

Why talents fail here: a Remain got absorbed, only one blocker stood in the Crushing lane, someone got hit during Hunting, the disarm took over a minute, two same-color puppets touched, or pairs got spent on knockdowns too late. Talents show on the results screen at the end.

### Liminal Ascension (Lv 100): before you queue

- Hard gate is level 100. Recommended Martial Mastery is listed as 34,000 in one guide (sample clear at ~36,800) and 3,400 in another, so check the in-game screen. It's a recommendation, not a lock.
- Roles to assign: two portal runners (Bai Chuyu), healers watching the runners, one or two pillar players for the math puzzles (Halcyon), and everyone else on Chirpy duty.
- Full rewards need every talent on both bosses. The end screen shows which talents registered.
- Controls: guides list Chirpy as the G key on PC. Console players: check your binding before the run.

### Bai Chuyu (Veiled Lady)

A gauge beside her HP bar opens portals: red portals at markers 1 and 3 when the first bar fills, blue at 2 and 4 when the second fills. She keeps 50 stacks of damage reduction until the runners win the illusion.

- Heaven Cleaver (red line) and Blazing Cleave (yellow circle): dodge every time. If targeted, aim the line into the Illusion Gate, which refreshes the runners' buff inside.
- Colossal Blade fires four cones. Heng Blade drops a big circle followed by arena-wide damage, so leave the circle early.
- Trap Generation: red cross on the floor. Runners stand on separate tips and get vined; everyone else breaks the vines. Freed runners get Blossoms, which they need to enter the red portal.
- Red portal: runners spread out (overlapping Blazing Circles is lethal) and kill the illusion. Reward: a skill that burns away vines, saved for when Colossal Blade lands on a vine-covered arena.
- Twin Vines: yellow markers are stack-and-share. Never soak one alone.
- Blue portal: runners stand in Spawning Fire to get Ablaze (it ticks damage, so heal them), then go in together and stay close during Vine Binding. Reward: an Ice Staff skill that raises barriers against Blazing Flame fireballs.

| Talent | Condition |
|---|---|
| 1-1 | No damage from Heaven Cleaver or Blazing Cleave |
| 1-2 | Win an illusion battle and get the extra skill |
| 1-3 | Win all illusions with no Vine Bind / Blazing Circle hits and no lost Blossoms or Ablaze |
| 2-1 | Guide Heaven Cleaver or Blazing Cleave into the Illusion Gate once |
| 2-2 | Share Twin Vines or block Blazing Flame fireballs twice |
| 2-3 | No solo Twin Vines hits and no unblocked fireballs |

### Halcyon

Everything here uses Chirpy: hold to swap polarity (float up from the ground or drop down from the air), tap to aim and fire a charge.

- Magnetic Pole Spread: lifts the raid. Hold Chirpy to drop back down before the blast.
- Magnetic Outburst: the opposite. Hold Chirpy to float and stay up until the cast ends.
- Azure Sword Shadow: three players get lifted and marked. Teammates shoot Chirpy charges at them to free them before the hit lands.
- Basic Algorithm: number pillars on left and right. Interact to raise numbers until both sides match, then get clear before they slam. Few hands on the pillars, or the count gets lost.
- Advanced Arithmetic: five pillars, same rule. For Talent 2-3, both sides must total exactly 9.
- Sky-Shattering Halcyon: she flies up behind a golden shield. The raid lands at least six Chirpy charges to pull her down, then burns the shield. Don't blow all your charges right before this.
- Iron Bull's Expel Mode: two bulls with the same polarity. Someone flips one with a Chirpy charge; the two targeted players line up so the bulls charge head-on into each other. Kill both within 15 seconds.

| Talent | Condition |
|---|---|
| 1-1 | No damage from Magnetic Storm or Vertical Swordcut |
| 1-2 | Iron Bulls die within 15 seconds |
| 1-3 | Never more than 5 seconds in Magnetic Repulsion |
| 2-1 | Collect 5 Chirpy Energy from Basic Algorithm mechanisms |
| 2-2 | No damage from charging Calculation Units |
| 2-3 | Both sides sum to 9 in Advanced Arithmetic |

### Snowfall's Reckoning (Lv 96): Zheng E + Snowplum Requiem

The newest Hero's Realm below level 100. Zheng E is an ice tower and parry fight; Snowplum Requiem is a sound-wave puzzle.

**Before you queue**

- Everyone equips the Lion's Roar mystic skill. Snowplum Requiem doesn't work without it.
- Recommended Martial Mastery is around 35,700 (30,000 baseline).
- Bring at least two healers (three makes Zheng E easier), some ranged damage, and fire arrows. Fire arrows are the only way to destroy the blue flowers.

| Role | Zheng E | Snowplum Requiem |
|---|---|---|
| Healers (2–3) | Alternate Cloudburst Healing to keep Frost Poison Resistance up. One climbs the tower during Cold Invasion | Keep the boss team alive |
| Ranged | Elites in the blue circle; fire arrows on blue flowers | On the boss |
| 3 guqin players | Normal duties | Play the guqin rhythm game during Three Performances |
| 3 protectors | Normal duties | Lion's Roar to shield the guqin players |
| Tank + the rest | Deflect Ice Blade Bursts, the Sleeping Puppet and charging elites | On the boss; Five Tones orbs |

**Zheng E**

- He jumps onto an ice tower with a shield and a blue Frost Poison circle around it. Never step into the blue circle without Frost Poison Resistance.
- Healers: Cloudburst Healing's cooldown is longer than its duration, so two healers must alternate. Casting at the same time leaves a gap that can wipe the raid. Elites drop Lotus Leaves that boost Cloudburst.
- Winter Vortex add: kill it fast or it pulls people into the hazard.
- Ice Blade Burst: step outside the circle and deflect. Four deflects empty his Qi and knock him off the tower for a damage window.
- Ice Vines Entangle: tethers two players. The whole team stacks on one spot to clear it. (A third player can also redirect an ice blade across the tether.)
- Ice Spike Pursuit: red circle on a player. That player runs away from the group until it ends.
- Cold Invasion: one healer climbs the tower and spams heals while everyone else breaks his gold shield in under 18 seconds. At the same time: deflect the Sleeping Puppet so it can't knock the healer off, shoot the blue flowers with fire arrows (they poison the tower healers, lethal at 20 stacks), and deflect any charging elite's red attack. Too slow and he restarts his mechanics.

| Talent | Condition |
|---|---|
| 1-1 | Never enter the Frost Poison area without Resistance |
| 1-2 | Deflect at least four Ice Blade Bursts |
| 1-3 | Whole team takes no Ice Blade Burst damage |
| 2-1 | Deflect the Sleeping Puppet during Cold Invasion |
| 2-2 | Shoot at least four blue flowers with fire arrows during Cold Invasion |
| 2-3 | No elite reaches the tower and the shield breaks within 18 seconds |

**Snowplum Requiem**

- Responding Melody: gold ring on you → face away from the group and use Lion's Roar just before the ring finishes charging to reflect the wave. Everyone else deflects the red AoEs.
- Soul Touching Melodies: vertical sound strings. Stand in the gaps between them, then heal through what follows.
- Five Tones: the central bell shows a target note. Hit the orbs around it to cycle their color Yellow → Green → Red → Blue → Orange, then send orange orbs into the bell with the interact button. Kill the Five-Chimes within 30 seconds. Wrong notes in the bell fail a talent.
- Three Performances: the three guqin players play a rhythm game and must play it perfectly to break her gold shield, or the raid wipes. Protectors Lion's Roar the gold Responding Melody waves away from them.

| Talent | Condition |
|---|---|
| 1-1 | Dodge every vertical sound wave in Soul Touching Melodies |
| 1-2 | Protect a guqin player with Lion's Roar at least once |
| 1-3 | Counter every Responding Melody in the fight |
| 2-1 | Send two correct notes in Five Tones |
| 2-2 | Five-Chimes dies within 30 seconds |
| 2-3 | All guqin players play perfectly and no wrong notes in Five Tones |

## Other endgame

- Taiping Mausoleum (S5 Path Trial, Phase 1): needs Foundation Trial Chapter 10 cleared; up to 60 stars of rewards; ends Nov 26. Mausoleum buffs now unlock in Basic, Mastery and Star groups.
- Arena (S5): 1v1, Group Strategy, 2v2, 3v3 and Perception Forest. Legend in any two non-Forest modes gets a face accessory; any four gets a pose.
- Transdust Ruins: extraction mode. Needs Lv 20 and the quest Sands of Perils; Solo and Trio are open all day.
- Skyward Bond: guild challenge for up to 10, with harder tiers added later.
- Guild Hero's Realm: boss talent tracking now records reward counts for both bosses.
- Guild War: runs on fixed server time and doesn't shift for daylight saving, so your local time can move an hour. Check the in-game panel.
- Campaign Challenges: martial art materials and targeted set gear (two set pieces per run).
- Engagement Realm: new season of progress rewards, including Relaying Stone: Dustless.
- Catch-up: Gear Enhance 56–60 has catch-up progression, and Tier 96 options are in the catch-up chests.
- Reflection Temple: closed until Jiangnan arrives; progress is kept.
